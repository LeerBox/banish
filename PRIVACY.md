# Banish — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Banish is a browser extension by **LeerBox** that adds a one-click "Not interested + Block" button to TikTok.

## What Banish collects
**Nothing.** Banish doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, and it has no servers of its own.

## What stays on your device
Banish uses Chrome's extension storage, on your device only, to keep:
- your settings, such as "Mark Not interested first" and "Auto-skip blocked accounts";
- a list of the TikTok usernames you blocked with Banish, with the time of each block, shown in the popup.

You can clear this list at any time from the popup (**Clear list**). Removing the extension deletes all of it.

## How Banish acts on TikTok
When you press the Banish button, the extension clicks TikTok's own buttons for you, in your own logged-in TikTok session:
- **Not interested** on the video;
- **⋯ → Block → Block** on the account's profile, which opens briefly in a hidden frame or a background tab.

Banish only runs on `https://www.tiktok.com/*`. It doesn't read your messages, passwords or payment details, and it doesn't send TikTok data anywhere else.

## Third parties
Banish has no analytics, ads or trackers, and it loads no remote code.
